COMMON QUESTIONS & GUIDANCE

Answers for pet parents.

Everything you need to know about preparing for your pet's visit, vaccinations, and clinic care at Vet Care LTD.

Do I need to book an appointment, or can I walk in? +

We warmly welcome walk-ins during our normal clinic hours, though scheduling an appointment ensures minimal waiting time. Critical pet emergencies always receive immediate triage priority.

What vaccinations does my dog or puppy need in Dar es Salaam? +

In Tanzania, core vaccinations for dogs include Rabies (legally required for public health) and the DHLPP combination vaccine protecting against Distemper, Hepatitis, Parvovirus, and Parainfluenza. Monthly tick and flea prevention is also vital for coastal health.

How do I know if my pet has an emergency? +

Signs of an acute emergency include difficulty breathing, severe lethargy, continuous vomiting, snakebite, suspected poisoning (rat bait, toxic chemicals), or physical trauma. Contact our emergency line at +255 713 325 000 immediately.

What payment methods are accepted at Vet Care LTD? +

Tunapokea njia zote za kawaida za malipo nchini Tanzania: M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Tigo Pesa, debit & credit cards (Visa & Mastercard), pamoja na pesa taslimu (cash).

Do you offer professional pet grooming and medicated baths? +

Yes! We provide full veterinary grooming services including medicated dermatological baths for skin conditions, anti-tick and anti-flea treatments, hygienic trims, ear flushing, and nail clipping.
